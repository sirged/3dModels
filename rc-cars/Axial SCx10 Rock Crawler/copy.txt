These are bumper brackets for the Axial SCX10. Made these so that I could extend both the front and back to fit a new body. Also added stabilizers to use towards the battery holder as I had to move mine to the front. The battery holders will have to be fit. I used a torch to fit them then superglued them to the bumper.
Once done you must drill out the main hole to 7.9mm, and the smaller holes to 2.4mm. These are the standard drill bits you can get from local hardware store. On the left and right side I just left a divot for you to drill the screw hole yourself to 2.4 mm as well. 

This will fit both the front and the back. The Green are the parts on the crawler.
